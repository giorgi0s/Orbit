# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS + Motion, shipped as a PWA (installed from Safari to the iPhone home screen and the Mac Dock), Supabase for data/auth/scheduled push, hosted on Vercel's free plan. Confirmed by the user. Must cost nothing to run.

## Users

One person: the owner. Not a product for anyone else, never sold. Currently plans nothing in advance and wants to get organised across every part of life: work, gym, food, home, and getting out of the room more.

## Product Purpose

A personal life planner that makes planning easy enough to actually happen. Success means the owner plans the week on Sunday, gets reminded before things happen, logs work hours per project, meal-preps from a plan, ticks off weekly goals, and goes to the gym and out of the house more often, and enjoys opening the app while doing it.

## Positioning

Built for exactly one person, so it can be as personal, odd and playful as they like. It covers their whole life in one place (calendar, work timer, meal prep, weekly goals, gym and going out) instead of five separate apps.

## Operating Context

- Opened at every time of day: mornings on the iPhone (often still in bed), during the workday on the MacBook with the work timer running beside other apps, evenings and in bed in low light, and a longer Sunday planning session.
- iPhone use is one-handed and quick; Mac use sits in a window next to work tools.
- Sunday "Weekly Reset" ritual: look back, pick 3–5 goals, block gym and going-out time, plan meals, brain-dump tasks.
- Work time entries get exported (CSV / copied text) into a separate work productivity tool.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Planned areas (see PLAN.md): Today screen, natural-language quick add, calendar with day-before / day-of / 1-hour push reminders, cross-device work timer with reports and export, weekly goals and weekday/weekend/someday tasks, meal prep (recipes, weekly meal grid, shopping list, fridge tracker), gym streak, "Touch Grass" going-out tracker, "Bored?" idea wheel, Sunday Weekly Reset, daily briefs.
- PWA limits: no home-screen widgets or Live Activities; push works only after installing to the home screen; haptics limited on iOS.
- Free-tier only: Supabase free plan (pauses after 7 idle days, mitigated by a daily ping), Vercel Hobby.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Orbit. Life areas are "planets"; the planning metaphor is things orbiting you.
- Voice: gently playful. Warm, encouraging and a bit silly; never sarcastic, never guilt-tripping.
- A small mascot the owner names themselves.
- Satisfying, delightful micro-interactions are a core requirement.
- Must never feel corporate / hustle-culture, and must never feel busy or cluttered.

## Evidence on Hand

No real data yet. All mockup content (events, projects, meals, goals) is illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. Adding something must take seconds, or it won't get added.
2. Calm first: show only what matters right now; everything else is one tap away.
3. Reward follow-through with delight, never punish with guilt.
4. Plan the week once, then let the app carry it.
5. It's theirs: personal quirks beat generic best practice.
