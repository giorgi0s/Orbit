# Today screen

Scope: the Today screen of Orbit (home tab), iPhone-first PWA, also used in a Mac window. Visitor mode: Operate.

Job: in a few seconds, see what's happening now, the rest of today in time order, today's to-dos and this week's goals, then tick things off or jot something new. Opened morning, during work (timer running), in bed at night, and on Sunday.

Content: running work timer (or tomorrow's first thing at night), schedule, to-dos with carried-over items, weekly goals with counts, Bean's line, quick-add. All mockup data is illustrative.

Constraints: calm and uncluttered; never corporate; gently playful voice; one-handed on iPhone; day and bedtime (navy) versions.

Reference mockup: mockups/today-vibes.html (Sticker Planner column).

Memorable moment: ticking a task stamps a puffy sticker onto the pen-drawn box and runs a highlighter through it.

Unresolved: mascot name (Bean is a placeholder); sticker set beyond star/heart/sun; whether sounds are on by default; how the planner tabs adapt to a wide Mac window.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Today is one page of a well-loved paper planner that never runs out of stickers. It refuses the category default (white cards, stock checkboxes, a neutral sans dashboard) and the literal space-theme reading of the name.

OWN-WORLD: Cool-white dot-grid page (#FCFCFE, 18px blue-grey dots), navy gel-pen ink #1C2554, a pink margin rule #FF6FAE, and highlighter swipes (lime #BEF446, sky, pink, orange) as the only emphasis. Puffy die-cut stickers (gold star, pink heart, lime sun) are the only full colour. One yellow sticky note #FFE45C held by pink washi tape. A green elastic strap #137A5F runs down the right edge, and coloured index tabs hang from the page edge onto a navy cover. Shantell Sans throughout: more informal for headings, steadier for data. Phosphor icons. Bedtime: the same page in navy #1A2045 with light ink.

STORY: Open Orbit, see what is happening now on the sticky note, read the day top to bottom with times in the margin, tick off to-dos, stamp the weekly sticker chart, and jot anything new on the line at the bottom.

FIRST VIEWPORT: "Monday" at 46px in handwriting with a lime highlighter swipe, the date beneath it, a gel-pen Bean doodle top right, and Bean's handwritten line under the date. Below that, the sticky note (rotated -1.3°, washi on top) with the running timer at 44px and a pen-outlined Stop button. Then "Today", with times in a 70px margin left of the pink rule. The primary action is the "Jot it down…" line pinned above the index tabs.

FORM: Sticker Planner, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list (cosy-game HUD, virtual pet device, sticker planner, lo-fi room window, fridge door, orrery, risograph sheet). Seed key f6bb8b44. Raised from the split-flap board (times in a fixed margin; the structure never reshuffles), the vertical feed (one dominant Now note) and the gate board (changed items stay highlighted until seen). Signature interaction: a sticker stamp with overshoot and slight rotation plus a soft thunk, then a highlighter sweep through the text. Motion grammar: springy stamps and tabs, ease-out sweeps, nothing scattered.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
